Extensions
==========

The most frequently used implementations of interfaces or individual functions in the developed `Telegram-bots` were combined and included as an integral part of the original `Telegram-Bot-Discussion` package.

One can argue for a long time about the correctness of this approach but let's accept it `AS IS`.
